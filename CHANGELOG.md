# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release: finite volume model of pressure, vacuum and
vacuum-pressure swing adsorption columns (PSA, VSA, VPSA), cycles to cyclic
steady state with coupled steps and vector acceleration, a data bank of
gases, problem specifications (`swingpy.spec`, with example input files and
setups in `examples`), Langmuir, Sips, Toth, extended Langmuir and IAST
isotherms, particle diffusion, a family of single-column VSA cycles
(`swingpy.cycles.vsa_cycle`) with presets for the seven cycle configurations
of Haghpanah et al. (2013), and a complete set of tables and figures of
every simulation.
