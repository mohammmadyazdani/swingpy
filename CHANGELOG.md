# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release: finite volume model of adsorption columns (vacuum,
pressure and temperature swing), cycles to cyclic steady state with coupled
steps and vector acceleration, a data bank of adsorbents and gases, problem
specifications (`swingpy.spec`, with example input files and setups in
`examples`), Langmuir, Sips, Toth, multisite Langmuir, virial and IAST
isotherms, humid gas, particle diffusion, a Peng-Robinson equation of state,
and a complete set of tables and figures of every simulation.
