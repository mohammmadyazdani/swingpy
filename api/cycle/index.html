    Cycles and streams¶

    swingpy.cycle ¶

    Cycles, cyclic steady state and key performance indicators.

    A cycle is a list of steps run one after the other on a single column; the state at the end of a step is the initial state of the next one. The key performance indicators are computed from the stream labels of the boundary conditions, so they work for any cycle: the purity and recovery of the key_component are taken in the stream labelled product against the streams labelled feed.

    CyclePerformance dataclass ¶

    Key performance indicators of one cycle.

    Attributes:

    Name Type Description
    purity float

    Mole fraction of the key component in the product stream, %.
    recovery float

    Key component in the product over key component fed, %.
    mass_balance_error float

    |in - out| / in of the key component over the cycle, %.
    specific_energy float

    Machine work per tonne of key component in the product, kWh/t.
    productivity float

    Key component in the product per adsorbent volume and cycle time, mol/(m3 s). The adsorbent volume is (1 - eps) A L.
    cycle_time float

    Sum of the actual step durations, s (steps with a stop condition may end early or be skipped).
    energy_by_step dict

    Machine work of each step, J.
    streams dict

    Net moles of each component in each labelled stream per cycle, positive out of the column.
    heat_by_step dict

    Heat supplied to the column through its wall in each step, J (negative when the column is cooled).
    residual float

    Normalized residual error of Subraveti, Karimi and Gazzani (2026, eq 2): over the steps, the largest 100 x sum of squares of the change of the dimensionless profiles at the end of the step from the previous cycle; filled in by Cycle.run.
    profile_change float

    Largest change of the column profiles over the cycle (dimensionless, see ColumnModel.profile_change); filled in by Cycle.run.

    KPIs of one cycle from the step results.

    run_once(model, state, buffers=None, n_output=50, record_points=200, fallback=True) ¶

    Simulate the steps once and return the list of StepResult.

    buffers holds the recorded streams (name to StreamRecord). Steps that read a record get it before they run, and outlets with a record_as name store their stream after they run, so a record made in one cycle is still available to steps earlier in the next cycle (for example the heavy reflux tank).

    run(model, initial_state, max_cycles=600, tolerance=0.5, consecutive=5, report_every=25, verbose=True, keep_cycles=10, criterion='both', profile_tolerance=0.001, accelerate=False, acceleration_memory=5, acceleration_start=None, acceleration_every=3, acceleration_stop=0.0001, residual_tolerance=1e-05, acceleration_max_jump=100.0) ¶

    Repeat the cycle until cyclic steady state.

    Two tests of steady state are available, both checked over consecutive cycles in a row:

    "mass_balance"  the key component mass balance error over a cycle
                is below ``tolerance`` (%), the usual criterion in
                the literature
"profiles"      no profile of the column changes by more than
                ``profile_tolerance`` over a cycle (dimensionless)
"both"          both of the above (default)
"residual"      the normalized residual error (eq 2 of Subraveti,
                Karimi and Gazzani 2026, ``CyclePerformance.residual``)
                is below ``residual_tolerance`` and the mass
                balance error below ``tolerance``, the criterion
                of that paper (use with ``consecutive=1``)

    The mass balance alone can be met while the column is still slowly warming or loading, so "both" is the safer choice; use "mass_balance" to apply only the usual mass balance rule.

    Acceleration (see swingpy.acceleration) starts some cycles from an extrapolated state instead of the end of the last cycle:

    accelerate="graves_morris"   or another vector Steffensen method of
                             ``acceleration.STEFFENSEN_METHODS``
                             (Subraveti, Karimi and Gazzani 2026):
                             ``acceleration_start`` plain cycles
                             (3 by default), then an extrapolation
                             every ``acceleration_every`` cycles
                             until the residual is below
                             ``acceleration_stop``; an extrapolation
                             that jumps more than
                             ``acceleration_max_jump`` times the last
                             cycle's change is discarded
accelerate=True or "anderson"  Anderson mixing of the last
                             ``acceleration_memory`` cycles, after
                             ``acceleration_start`` (5 by default)

    The KPIs and the steady state tests are still those of simulated cycles. The step results of the last keep_cycles cycles are kept in CycleRun.recent.

    swingpy.cycles ¶

    A family of single-column VSA cycles built from optional steps.

    vsa_cycle assembles one cycle from the steps of the cycle synthesis of Haghpanah et al. (2013), AIChE J. 59, 4735, https://doi.org/10.1002/aic.14192 (their Figures 2 and 6):

    FEED   adsorption at P_H; the raffinate is recorded
BLOW   co-current blowdown to P_I            (depressurization="blowdown")
EQDN   co-current pressure equalization       (depressurization="equalization")
HREF   heavy reflux at P_I, optional: a fraction of the extract enters
       the feed end at a constant molar flow while the product end is
       held at P_I; the refluxed gas is subtracted from the product
EVAC   counter-current evacuation to P_L; recorded as the extract
LREF   light reflux at P_L, optional: raffinate purges the column from
       the product end, the gas leaving the feed end is product
EQUP   receiving side of the equalization (depressurization="equalization")
LPPR   light product pressurization with the raffinate, then PRES with
       feed to P_H                            (pressurization="light product")
PRES   pressurization with feed               (pressurization="feed")

    The seven cycles of the article are presets of this function in this module (HAGHPANAH_2013).

    Pressure changes are either prescribed, as in the article (an exponential approach at rate over a given step time), or driven by vacuum pumps of given interstitial velocity, ended when the pressure is reached (give blowdown_velocity or evacuation_velocity). The equalization is always the single column treatment: the donor follows a prescribed pressure history and its outflow is recorded, the receiver takes that recorded flow in through its product end.

    The function builds the cycle only; the column model (pressure drop law, mass transfer, heat transfer) is chosen independently when the cycle is run.

    HAGHPANAH_2013 = {'basic four step (Figure 2)': basic_four_step, 'four step with LPP (Figure 6a)': four_step_lpp, 'five step with LPP and LR (Figure 6b)': five_step_lpp_lr, 'six step with LPP, LR and HR (Figure 6c)': six_step_lpp_lr_hr, 'five step with PE and FP (Figure 6d)': five_step_pe_fp, 'five step with PE and LPP (Figure 6e)': five_step_pe_lpp, 'six step with PE, LPP and HR (Figure 6f)': six_step_pe_lpp_hr} module-attribute ¶

    The seven cycle configurations of Haghpanah et al. (2013), by name.

    basic_four_step(t_ads, t_bd, t_evac, intermediate, low, feed_velocity, t_press=20.0) ¶

    Basic four-step cycle of Figure 2, run in the order FEED, BLOW, EVAC, PRES.

    The article starts every cycle from a column at 1 bar. Feed pressurization starts from P_L, so beginning with it would expose the 1 bar column to a 0.03 bar face for no physical reason. Starting with adsorption gives the same cyclic steady state without that first shock.

    four_step_lpp(t_ads, t_bd, t_evac, intermediate, low, feed_velocity, pressure_limited_lpp=True) ¶

    Four-step cycle with light product pressurization, Figure 6a: FEED, BLOW, EVAC, LPPR, PRES.

    Light product pressurization uses the raffinate of the feed step, which can enter at most at P_H, the pressure it is produced at. The step ends when the closed feed end reaches 0.99 P_H, at most after t_ads; a feed pressurization step (PRES) then completes the pressurization. With pressure_limited_lpp=False the recorded flow is imposed whatever the pressure and the step ends at P_H, the unibed treatment of the article, which over-pressurizes the product end.

    five_step_lpp_lr(t_ads, t_bd, t_evac, t_lr, intermediate, low, feed_velocity, pressure_limited_lpp=True) ¶

    Five-step cycle with light product pressurization and light reflux, Figure 6b: FEED, BLOW, EVAC, LREF, LPPR. The first t_LR seconds of the raffinate purge the column at P_L, the rest pressurizes it.

    six_step_lpp_lr_hr(t_ads, t_bd, t_hr, t_evac, t_lr, intermediate, low, reflux_ratio, feed_velocity, pressure_limited_lpp=True) ¶

    Six-step cycle with light product pressurization, light and heavy reflux, Figure 6c: FEED, BLOW, HREF, EVAC, LREF, LPPR. A fraction reflux_ratio of the evacuation stream is fed back at the feed end during HREF, at a constant flow (eq. 8 of the article), while a vacuum pump holds the product end at P_I. The refluxed gas is subtracted from the product.

    five_step_pe_fp(t_ads, t_pe, t_evac, low, feed_velocity, t_press=20.0, kappa=0.041) ¶

    5-step cycle with one pressure equalization and feed pressurization, Figure 6d: FEED, EQDN, EVAC, EQUP, PRES.

    The two columns of the equalization are coupled through a recorded stream: the donor blows down from P_H through its product end to the intermediate pressure of eq. 9 of the article, P_I = (P_H + P_L) / 2 - kappa with kappa = 0.041 bar, and the receiver takes in the recorded gas through its product end. The receiver then ends at P_REC, which the article reports to lie 0.01 to 0.1 bar below P_I, as it should for gas flowing from the donor to the receiver. Feed pressurization then completes the pressurization to P_H.

    five_step_pe_lpp(t_ads, t_pe, t_evac, low, feed_velocity, kappa=0.041, pressure_limited_lpp=True) ¶

    5-step cycle with one pressure equalization and light product pressurization, Figure 6e: FEED, EQDN, EVAC, EQUP, LPPR (then PRES to P_H).

    The equalization is that of five_step_pe_fp; light product pressurization replaces feed pressurization from P_REC to P_H.

    six_step_pe_lpp_hr(t_ads, t_pe, t_hr, t_evac, low, reflux_ratio, feed_velocity, kappa=0.041, pressure_limited_lpp=True) ¶

    6-step cycle with pressure equalization, light product pressurization and heavy reflux, Figure 6f: FEED, EQDN, HREF, EVAC, EQUP, LPPR (then PRES).

    The heavy reflux follows the equalization at its end pressure P_I, as in six_step_lpp_lr_hr: a fraction reflux_ratio of the extract enters the feed end while a vacuum pump holds the product end at P_I.

    swingpy.streams ¶

    Recorded gas streams that couple one step of a cycle to another.

    In a multi-bed plant the effluent of one column feeds another one at the same time: the raffinate of the adsorption step pressurizes a column (light product pressurization) or purges it (light reflux), and part of the extract is sent back to the feed end (heavy reflux). SwingPy simulates a single column, so such a stream is recorded while one step runs and played back when the receiving step runs (the single-bed or "unibed" approach). Records are replayed interval by interval, conserving the net recorded moles (gas that flowed back into the source column is not replayed).

    StreamRecord dataclass ¶

    Time history of a gas stream leaving a column end.

    Attributes:

    Name Type Description
    time ndarray

    Time from the start of the source step, s.
    molar_flow ndarray

    Total molar flow, mol/s (positive).
    mole_fractions ndarray

    Composition, shape (n_components, len(time)).
    temperature ndarray

    K.
    pressure ndarray

    Face pressure, bar (used when another step needs the pressure history of this one, for example a valve venting into it).
    cumulative ndarray

    Moles of each component that have left since the start of the step, at each time, exactly as the model integrated them (shape components x times). When given, total_moles uses it rather than integrating the sampled flow, which misses or exaggerates sharp transients such as the first instants of an evacuation.

    pressure_at(time_s) ¶

    Face pressure at a time, bar (held at the ends of the record).

    mean_composition() ¶

    Flow-weighted mean composition of the stream.

    replayable_increments() ¶

    Moles of each component available for replay in each sampling interval (components x intervals).

    Gas can flow back into the source column through the recorded end, for example at the start of a feed step that begins below the outlet pressure. That gas comes out of the same stream, so it is subtracted from what left before or after it: the replayable moles follow the running maximum of the net moles that have left, never negative. A replay therefore never returns more than the net moles the source delivered, and returns exactly the recorded moles when no gas flowed back.

    at(time_s) ¶

    Molar flow (mol/s), composition and temperature at a time; zero flow outside the record.

    With cumulative the flow is the mean over the sampling interval that contains the time (moles that left in the interval over its length), so that a replay returns exactly the recorded moles even when the source step starts with a sharp transient (a pressure equalization) that the sampled flow cannot resolve. Without it the sampled flow and composition are interpolated linearly.

    total_moles(start=0.0, end=None) ¶

    Moles of each component between two times, mol.

    swingpy.acceleration ¶

    Faster approach to cyclic steady state by vector acceleration of the cycle map.

    Two families are available:

    • SteffensenAccelerator: the vector extensions of Steffensen's method compared for adsorption cycles by Subraveti, Karimi and Gazzani, Ind. Eng. Chem. Res. 65 (2026) 9656, https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.iecr.6c00407 (Table 1), with their schedule: plain cycles first, then an extrapolation every few cycles, and plain cycles only once the residual is small. Their best method overall is Graves-Morris.
    • AndersonAccelerator: Anderson mixing of the last few cycles.

    Anderson acceleration:

    One cycle maps the state of the column at its start to the state at its end, s -> F(s); cyclic steady state is the fixed point s = F(s). Repeating the cycle converges linearly, and slow modes (a column that keeps warming or loading a little each cycle) can take hundreds of cycles. Anderson acceleration combines the last few iterates to extrapolate towards the fixed point: with residuals g_k = F(s_k) - s_k it finds the weights that minimise the norm of their combination and moves to the same combination of the F(s_k).

    Safeguards: the extrapolated state is projected back into the physical range (mole fractions between 0 and 1, positive pressures, loadings and temperatures), and whenever a step makes the residual grow markedly the history is dropped and a plain cycle follows. The performance indicators and the steady state tests are always those of a simulated cycle, never of an extrapolated state.

    SteffensenAccelerator ¶

    Vector Steffensen extrapolation of the cycle map (Subraveti, Karimi and Gazzani 2026).

    The schedule is that of the paper: start plain cycles, an extrapolation at the next one, then an extrapolation after every every plain cycles; once the residual of a cycle falls below stop_residual no further extrapolation is made, so the last cycles to steady state are plain ones. Each extrapolation uses the last two plain cycles: D -> F(D) -> F(F(D)).

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    model

    The ColumnModel (for the layout of the state).

    		 required
    method

    A key of STEFFENSEN_METHODS ("graves_morris" by default, the best method of the paper).

    		 'graves_morris'
    start

    Plain cycles before the first extrapolation (3 in the paper).

    		 3
    every

    Plain cycles between two extrapolations (3 in the paper, at least 2).

    		 3
    stop_residual

    Residual (Cycle.residual) below which no more extrapolations are made (1e-4 in the paper).

    		 0.0001
    max_jump

    Safeguard, not in the paper: an extrapolation that moves the state more than max_jump times the change over the last plain cycle (|new - F(D)| > max_jump |u|) is discarded and the plain cycle kept. The scalar parameter of every variant is a ratio of inner products whose denominator can pass through zero (for Zienkiewicz-Lohner v.w): on the zeolite 13X VSA case one extrapolation had lambda = -1980, a jump of 1900 |u|, and the next cycle could not be integrated. A slow mode with a contraction factor a needs a jump of about 1 / (1 - a), so the default 100 keeps modes down to a = 0.99.

    		 100.0
