    Boundary conditions and steps¶

    swingpy.boundaries ¶

    Boundary conditions at the two ends of the column.

    A cycle step is defined by what happens at the feed end (Z = 0) and at the product end (Z = 1). Each end is described by one of the classes below, so a new step is built by combining two of them rather than by editing the model. The same class works at either end: for example PressureOutlet at the product end is a blowdown, and at the feed end it is an evacuation.

    User inputs are in practical units: pressures in bar, velocities in m/s (interstitial), temperatures in K, compositions as dicts or sequences of mole fractions. The model converts them internally.

    Every boundary also says what kind of stream it is (stream), which the cycle uses to compute purity and recovery, and which machine moves the gas (a compressor fed from supply_pressure, or a vacuum pump discharging to discharge_pressure), which the cycle uses to compute energy.

    Efficiency = Union[float, Callable[[float], float]] module-attribute ¶

    A machine efficiency: a number, or a function of the suction pressure in bar.

    STANDARD_MOLAR_VOLUME = 0.022414 module-attribute ¶

    Molar volume of an ideal gas at 0 C and 1 atm, m3/mol, for flows given in standard litres.

    Constant ¶

    A pressure that does not change during the step, in bar.

    ExponentialRamp dataclass ¶

    Pressure relaxing exponentially from start to end (bar):

    P(t) = end + (start - end) exp(-rate t)

    a common way of prescribing the pressure history of pressurization, blowdown and evacuation steps, with rate the constant lambda in 1/s.

    LinearRamp dataclass ¶

    Pressure changing linearly from start to end (bar) over duration (s), then held at end:

    P(t) = start + (end - start) min(t / duration, 1)

    as used for the feed pressurization of Nguyen et al. (Chem. Eng. J. 452 (2023) 139550, Eq. 9).

    FaceState ¶

    Bases: NamedTuple

    Dimensionless velocity, pressure, independent mole fractions and temperature at one end face.

    Boundary ¶

    Base class of all end conditions.

    Attributes:

    Name Type Description
    role

    "closed", "inlet" or "outlet". Fixes the flow direction of the step and how the stream is counted in the cycle balances.
    stream

    Label of the stream, for example "feed", "product", "raffinate" or "waste". Purity and recovery are computed from these labels.
    supply_pressure

    For inlets, the pressure the gas is available at (bar); a compressor is needed above it.
    discharge_pressure

    For outlets, the pressure the gas is delivered to (bar); a vacuum pump is needed below it.
    efficiency Efficiency

    Efficiency of that machine (number or function of the suction pressure in bar).

    Closed ¶

    Bases: Boundary

    A closed valve: no flow, and zero gradients of pressure, composition and temperature.

    InstantFill ¶

    Bases: Boundary

    Feed end of an instantaneous pressurization (steps.instant_pressurization): the gas in the column is replaced at once by a gas of the given composition and pressure, the solid (loadings and temperature) frozen. Not a boundary of a simulated step: ColumnModel.simulate applies it directly.

    FeedInlet ¶

    Bases: Boundary

    Gas entering at a fixed interstitial velocity (flow controlled feed).

    The face pressure follows from the pressure drop over half a cell, and composition and temperature from the Danckwerts conditions at the inlet.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    velocity

    Interstitial velocity at the face, m/s.

    		 required
    composition

    Mole fractions of the entering gas (dict or sequence), or the name of a recorded stream (record_as): the gas is then its mean composition, as from a well mixed tank.

    		 required
    temperature

    Temperature of the entering gas, K.

    		 298.15
    supply_pressure

    Pressure the gas is available at, bar. Energy is charged for compressing it up to the face pressure.

    		 1.0
    efficiency Efficiency

    Compressor efficiency.

    		 0.72
    stream

    Stream label, "feed" by default.

    		 'feed'
    default_composition

    With a recorded stream, the gas used until the record exists (the first cycle).

    		 None

    PressureOutlet ¶

    Bases: Boundary

    Gas leaving while the face pressure is held or ramped: a back pressure regulator (constant pressure) or a vacuum pump following a prescribed pressure history (blowdown and evacuation).

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    pressure

    Face pressure in bar, a number or a history.

    		 required
    discharge_pressure

    Pressure the gas is delivered to, bar. Energy is charged for pumping from the face pressure up to it.

    		 1.0
    efficiency Efficiency

    Vacuum pump efficiency, a number or a function of the suction pressure such as falling_vacuum_efficiency.

    		 0.72
    stream

    Stream label, "waste" by default; use "product" for the stream whose purity and recovery are reported.

    		 'waste'
    record_as

    Name under which the outgoing stream is recorded, so that a later step can use it (BufferInlet, TankInlet).

    		 None
    check_valve

    Let gas only leave: while the column is below the set pressure the face stays closed (for example while the feed brings a column back up from sub-atmospheric pressure).

    		 False

    BufferInlet ¶

    Bases: Boundary

    Gas entering from a recorded stream, replayed in time: the molar flow, composition and temperature follow the record of another step. Used for light product pressurization and light reflux, where the raffinate of the adsorption step enters the column at the product end.

    The molar flow is imposed, so the velocity follows from v P / T at the face (equivalently v = v_source P_source / P). When the record is exhausted or not yet available (first cycle), no gas enters.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    buffer

    Name of the record (record_as of the source outlet).

    		 required
    offset

    Time into the record at which the replay starts, s. Lets two steps share one record, for example light reflux using the first t_LR seconds of the raffinate and pressurization the rest.

    		 0.0
    stream

    Stream label of the recycled gas.

    		 'raffinate'
    max_pressure

    Pressure of the source, bar. Gas cannot enter a column at a higher pressure than the one it is supplied at, so when the recorded flow would need a higher face pressure, the face is held at max_pressure and the flow follows from the pressure drop instead (it falls to zero as the column approaches that pressure). Without it the recorded flow is imposed whatever the pressure, which over-pressurizes the column near the end of a light product pressurization.

    		 None
    composition

    Replay only the recorded molar flow, with this gas instead of the recorded one (mole fractions, dict or sequence), for example a single column simulation of pressure equalization with pure product gas (Ribeiro et al., Chem. Eng. Sci. 63 (2008) 5258). None (default) replays the recorded composition.

    		 None

    TankInlet ¶

    Bases: Boundary

    Gas entering at a constant molar flow from a well mixed tank, used for heavy reflux: a fraction of the extract of a recorded step is sent back to the feed end over the duration of this step. The composition is the mean composition of the recorded stream.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    buffer

    Name of the record that fills the tank.

    		 required
    fraction

    Fraction of the recorded moles fed back (reflux ratio).

    		 required
    temperature

    Temperature of the gas leaving the tank, K.

    		 298.15
    supply_pressure

    Tank pressure, bar; a compressor is charged above it.

    		 1.0
    efficiency Efficiency

    Compressor efficiency.

    		 0.72
    stream

    Stream label, "product" by default, so that the refluxed gas is subtracted from the product.

    		 'product'

    VacuumPump ¶

    Bases: Boundary

    A vacuum pump of constant volumetric flow drawing gas out of the column.

    The pump fixes the actual volumetric flow at the column end, and hence the interstitial velocity v = S / (eps A), while the face pressure follows from the pressure drop over half a cell. How long it takes to reach a given vacuum is then a result of the simulation rather than an input, so the step is usually ended with stop=PressureReached(...). This gives more realistic step times and pump sizes than prescribed pressure histories.

    The pump is given either by its capacity or by the interstitial velocity it draws. The velocity keeps its meaning when an optimizer changes the column diameter; the capacity is then computed from it.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    volumetric_flow

    Pump capacity at suction conditions, m3/h.

    		 None
    velocity

    Interstitial velocity at the column end, m/s (instead of volumetric_flow).

    		 None
    discharge_pressure

    Pressure the gas is delivered to, bar.

    		 1.01325
    efficiency Efficiency

    Pump efficiency, a number or a function of the suction pressure such as falling_vacuum_efficiency.

    		 0.72
    stream

    Stream label.

    		 'waste'
    record_as

    Name under which the pumped stream is recorded.

    		 None

    interstitial_velocity(model) ¶

    Velocity drawn at the column end, m/s.

    capacity(model) ¶

    Pump capacity at suction conditions, m3/h.

    FlowInlet ¶

    Bases: Boundary

    Gas entering at a fixed molar flow, as set by a mass flow controller.

    The velocity follows from the flow and the face pressure; composition and temperature from the Danckwerts conditions. With max_pressure the face cannot rise above the supply pressure: once the flow would need a higher face pressure, the face is held there and the flow follows from the pressure drop (a pressurization that stops at the supply pressure).

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    molar_flow

    mol/s (use standard_litres_per_minute for flows in L/min STP).

    		 required
    composition

    Mole fractions of the entering gas, or the name of a recorded stream whose mean composition enters.

    		 required
    default_composition

    Composition used while that record does not exist yet.

    		 None
    temperature

    K.

    		 298.15
    max_pressure

    Supply pressure, bar, or None for no limit.

    		 None
    supply_pressure

    Pressure of the supply, bar; a compressor is charged above it.

    		 None
    efficiency Efficiency

    Efficiency of that compressor.

    		 0.72
    stream

    Stream label.

    		 'feed'

    MixingInlet ¶

    Bases: Boundary

    Fresh gas at a fixed molar flow mixed with gas recycled from a well mixed tank, for a recycle into the feed: the outlet of an earlier step is collected (record_as) and sent back, spread evenly over this step, together with the fresh feed.

    The recycled gas enters at temperature like the fresh gas (it is cooled to the feed temperature). The fresh moles count as the stream stream and the recycled ones as recycle_stream, so that recovery is computed on the fresh feed only and the recycle nets out against the step it came from (label that outlet with the same recycle_stream).

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    molar_flow

    Fresh gas, mol/s.

    		 required
    composition

    Mole fractions of the fresh gas.

    		 required
    buffer

    Name of the record that fills the tank.

    		 required
    fraction

    Fraction of the recorded moles sent back.

    		 1.0
    temperature

    Temperature of the mixture, K.

    		 298.15
    supply_pressure

    Pressure of the supply, bar; a compressor is charged above it.

    		 None
    efficiency Efficiency

    Efficiency of that compressor.

    		 0.72
    stream

    Label of the fresh gas ("feed").

    		 'feed'
    recycle_stream

    Label of the recycled gas ("recycle").

    		 'recycle'

    stream_moles(model, moles_in, duration) ¶

    Moles that entered, split into the fresh feed and the recycle.

    RecordedPressure ¶

    A reservoir pressure taken from the face pressure that another step recorded (record_as), typically in the previous cycle.

    In a multi-bed plant a bed that equalizes or supplies purge vents into another bed, whose pressure changes during the step. A single-column simulation records the face pressure of the receiving step and uses it as the downstream pressure of the donor step one cycle later; at cyclic steady state the two are consistent.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    buffer

    Name of the record.

    		 required
    default

    Pressure in bar before any record exists (first cycle), a number or a function of time in s.

    		 required

    Valve ¶

    Bases: Boundary

    A linear valve between the column end and a reservoir,

    molar flow = cv * (upstream pressure - downstream pressure),

    the flow law of the valves of most cycle simulators. The face pressure and flow are solved together with the pressure drop over half a cell.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    cv

    Valve coefficient, mol/(s bar).

    		 required
    pressure

    Reservoir pressure in bar: a number, a function of time in s, or a RecordedPressure (the pressure of another step).

    		 required
    direction

    "out" for gas leaving the column (the reservoir is downstream), "in" for gas entering it from the reservoir.

    		 'out'
    composition

    Gas of the reservoir, for "in".

    		 None
    temperature

    Temperature of the reservoir gas, K, for "in".

    		 298.15
    extra_outflow

    A fixed molar flow (mol/s) leaving through the same end in addition to the valve flow, for "out" (for example product sent to repressurize another bed).

    		 0.0
    reversible

    Allow flow against direction (the reservoir gas then enters, or the column gas leaves). Off by default.

    		 False
    stream

    Stream label.

    		 None
    record_as

    Name under which the flow is recorded.

    		 None

    as_history(pressure) ¶

    Accept a number (constant pressure) or any callable of time.

    falling_vacuum_efficiency(pressure_bar) ¶

    Vacuum pump efficiency that falls at deep vacuum, eta = 15.84 P / (1 + 19.80 P) with P the suction pressure in bar: about 0.72 at 0.1 bar or more, and much lower below 0.05 bar, as real pumps do.

    standard_litres_per_minute(flow) ¶

    Molar flow in mol/s of a gas flow given in standard litres per minute (0 C, 1 atm).

    swingpy.steps ¶

    Cycle steps.

    A step is the pair of boundary conditions at the two column ends, a duration, and optionally the ambient temperature of the column wall during the step (which turns a pressure swing cycle into a temperature swing one).

    Step dataclass ¶

    One step of a cycle.

    Attributes:

    Name Type Description
    name str

    Display name, for example "adsorption".
    duration float

    Step time, s.
    feed_end Boundary

    Boundary condition at Z = 0.
    product_end Boundary

    Boundary condition at Z = 1.
    ambient_temperature float

    Temperature outside the wall during this step, K. None keeps the column's own ambient temperature.
    stop object

    Optional condition that ends the step early, such as PressureReached; duration is then the longest it may last. A step whose condition already holds at its start is skipped.

    direction property ¶

    +1 when the gas flows from the feed end towards the product end, -1 for the opposite direction. Used to pick the upwind side of the face reconstruction. A step with both ends closed counts as +1.

    PressureReached dataclass ¶

    Ends a step when the pressure at a location reaches a target.

    Attributes:

    Name Type Description
    pressure float

    Target, bar.
    location str

    "feed" (first cell), "product" (last cell) or "mean".
    rising bool

    True when the pressure rises to the target (pressurization), False when it falls to it (blowdown, evacuation).

    value(model, state) ¶

    Zero at the target, positive once it is reached.

    instant_pressurization(name, composition, pressure, stream='feed') ¶

    A pressurization taken as instantaneous with the solid frozen: the gas in the column becomes composition at pressure (bar) at once, with the loadings and temperatures unchanged. Used when the pressurization is much faster than the uptake, for example in direct air capture (Balasubramaniam et al., Chem. Eng. J. 485 (2024) 149568: "the column is considered to be pressurized and filled with ambient air instantaneously ... the solid phase is assumed to be frozen").

    The moles that enter count as the stream stream (the feed by default); no compression work is charged.
